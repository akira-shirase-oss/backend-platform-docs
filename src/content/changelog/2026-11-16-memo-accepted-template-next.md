B0's evidence answered the question it was built for: the sibling products repeat a
*slice* — invite-gated accounts, sessions, workspaces, tokens — that their independence
rule forbids a shared service from supplying. The memo recommended staying parked and
shipping the slice as code each product copies and owns. The maintainer accepted the
memo with corrections, and the copy-and-own starter template (B1-T) becomes the next
milestone. The control plane's remaining milestones stay planned until explicitly
unparked.
